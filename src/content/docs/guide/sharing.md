---
title: Sharing a title
description: Share links open a title in Honya for anyone who has it, and on its site for anyone who doesn't.
---

Tap **Share** on a title's page to send a link to it by Messages, Mail or anything else in the share sheet.

## What the person you send it to sees

The link opens differently depending on what they have:

| They have | The link opens |
|---|---|
| Honya, with the title's source installed | The title's page in Honya, over whatever they were doing. |
| Honya, without that source | A message saying the title is on a source that isn't installed, with **Open in Browser** to see it on the source's site. Once they add the source in **Settings → Sources**, the link opens in Honya. |
| No Honya, on an iPhone or iPad | A page on this website with the title's name, where to get Honya, and a button to continue to the title's page on its source's site. After installing Honya, they can tap the link again to open it there. |
| No Honya, on anything else | The same page, with the source's site first. |

Nothing happens on its own on that page: it only goes to the site if they tap the button, and it only ever goes to an ordinary web page.

## What a link contains

A share link holds just enough to find the title again: which source it's on, the title's address on that source, its name, and its page on the source's site. It doesn't say who shared it or carry anything from your library. Developers can read the details in [Share links](/developers/share-links/).
