## History

**History**, which the clock on the Library tab opens, lists the chapters you've read, newest first, with the page you stopped on. Tap one to carry on from there, or swipe it to remove it from your history.

## Excluded categories

**Excluded Categories**, under History in **Settings → Privacy**, leaves the titles in the categories you pick out of History, the [Now reading](/guide/library/#now-reading) bar and [Spotlight](/guide/library/#spotlight). Reading them still saves your place and marks chapters read, so **Continue** on a title's page picks up where you stopped. Pick **Uncategorized** for titles in no category. Take a category off the list and its titles come back.

Titles you haven't added to your library always show. Like your other settings, this syncs through iCloud and is backed up.

## Clear history

**Settings → Privacy → Clear History** removes your reading history. Chapters you've finished stay marked as read, so your library still knows where you are.

## Incognito mode

Turn on **Settings → Privacy → Incognito Mode** to read without Honya remembering. While it's on, Honya doesn't save reading history, progress or recent searches on this device, doesn't update your [MyAnimeList](/guide/myanimelist/) list, and doesn't offer what you're reading to your other devices through [Handoff](/guide/reader/#handoff).

While incognito mode is on, the Settings tab's gear wears an eye-slash badge, so you can tell at a glance.

## Widgets

Honya has two widgets for your home screen, each a row of up to four covers:

- **Continue Reading** shows the titles in your library you read most recently, leaving out ones you've caught up on. Tap a cover to pick up where you stopped, or anywhere else to open your library.
- **Updates** shows the titles with the newest chapters, with the same count of new chapters as the bell on the Library tab. Tap a cover to open the title, or anywhere else to open Updates.

They catch up when you leave Honya, and when an update finds new chapters.

**Excluded Categories**, under Widgets in **Settings → Privacy**, keeps the titles in the categories you pick off both widgets. Pick **Uncategorized** for titles in no category. Titles left out of History stay off Continue Reading too. Like your other settings, this syncs through iCloud and is backed up.

## App Lock

Turn on App Lock in **Settings → Privacy** to need Face ID, Touch ID or your device's passcode to get into Honya. The switch is named after the way your device unlocks, such as **Require Face ID**.

- Honya is locked each time it's opened, and when you come back to it after the time you choose in **Lock**: immediately, after 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or after an hour.
- If it doesn't ask by itself, tap **Unlock with Face ID** (or Touch ID, or Passcode) on the lock screen.
- While it's on, the app switcher shows a cover in place of what's on screen, and notifications about new chapters don't name titles.
- The widgets show **Honya is locked** in place of covers, [Spotlight](/guide/library/#spotlight) doesn't list your titles, and [Handoff](/guide/reader/#handoff) isn't offered.
- It needs a passcode on your device. Without one, set it in the Settings app first.

Like incognito mode, App Lock is set on each device. It isn't synced through iCloud, and backups don't include it.
