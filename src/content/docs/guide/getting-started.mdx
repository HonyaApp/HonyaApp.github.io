Honya is a manga reader for iPhone and iPad, on iOS and iPadOS 18 or later. It's free, with no account to make.

Honya comes with no manga and no sources. What you read comes from **sources**, small scripts that each read one website, which other people write and publish in **repositories**. So the first thing to do is add a repository you trust.

## Set up

<Steps>

1. **Add a repository.** Go to **Settings → Sources** and tap **Add Repository**. The first time, Honya explains what third-party content means: repositories are made by people who aren't affiliated with Honya, and the sites their sources read from may include mature content. Tap **Agree and Continue** if you're happy to go on.

   Then enter the repository's address. That can be the address of its folder, its `index.json`, or a link to a GitHub repository. See [Adding a repository](/sources/repositories/) for the forms an address can take.

2. **Get some sources.** The repository's sources are listed with the ones in your languages first. Tap **Get** next to each one you want. Sources marked **18+** can show adult content.

3. **Find something to read.** Open the **Discover** tab and choose a source to browse it, or tap **Search** to look through your library and every source at once.

4. **Add it to your library.** On a title's page, tap **Add to Library**. If you have categories, Honya asks which ones it goes in, or uses your default category.

5. **Read.** Tap a chapter, or the **Continue** button, and Honya opens where you left off.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Coming from Mihon, Tachiyomi or Paperback?">
Make a backup there and open it in Honya. Your library, reading progress, categories and history come across, and the sources it used are found again where your repositories offer them. See [Backup and restore](/guide/backup-and-restore/).
</Aside>

## Find your way around

Honya has four tabs:

- **Library**: the titles you follow, in your categories. The bell opens **Updates**, where new chapters show up.
- **Discover**: a daily queue of titles picked for you, the manga behind this season's anime, and your sources to browse.
- **Settings**: sources, downloads, the reader's defaults, iCloud Sync, backups and the rest.
- **Search**: your library and every source, from one field.
