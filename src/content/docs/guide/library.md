The **Library** tab holds every title you've added, from all your sources, in one place.

## Categories

Categories split your library into tabs. Make and arrange them in **Settings → Library → Categories**. A title can be in more than one category, and titles in none of them are in **Uncategorized**.

- **Default Category** decides where titles go when you add them. Choose **Always Ask** to pick each time.
- To move titles, select them in the library and choose **Edit Categories**, or use **Edit Categories** on a title's page.
- Deleting a category asks where its titles should go. Titles also in other categories stay in those.

With iCloud Sync on, your categories and default category are the same on all your devices.

## Sort, filter and display

Tap **Options** in the library to change how it looks.

**Filter** narrows the list, for example to titles with unread or downloaded chapters. Tap a filter once to show only those titles, again to hide them, and a third time to clear it.

**Sort** orders the list by title, last read, latest chapter, unread count, total chapters or date added. Tap the chosen order again to reverse it.

**Display** chooses a layout (compact grid, comfortable grid, or covers only) and which badges covers wear: unread chapters, downloaded chapters, and the **Continue Reading** button. **Category Counts** shows how many titles each category holds.

Sorting and filters can be set for every category at once. The number of columns, which applies to covers everywhere in the app, is in **Settings → Appearance**.

## Now reading

Once you've read something, a **Now reading** bar sits above the tab bar on the library. Tap it to go straight back to the title you were reading. On a title's page, the same place holds its **Continue** button.

## A title's page

A title's page has its details, chapters and everything you can do with it:

- **Chapters** can be sorted by source order, chapter number, upload date or name, and filtered to read, downloaded or bookmarked chapters. **Chapter Numbers as Titles** shows "Chapter 12" in place of each chapter's own name.
- Swipe a chapter to mark it read, bookmark it, download it, or mark every chapter before it read or unread.
- **Notes** keep anything you want to remember about a title. They travel with it through backups and migration.
- **Page bookmarks** you save in the reader are listed on the title's page.
- **Open in WebView** shows the title on its source's site, where you can sign in or solve a check the site asks for.
- **Share** sends a link to the title. See [Sharing a title](/guide/sharing/).
- **Migrate** moves the title to another source. See [Migration](/guide/migration/).

### Custom covers

While reading, long-press a page and choose **Set as Cover** to use it as the title's cover everywhere in the app. **Reset Cover** on the title's page goes back to the source's.

## Duplicates and other names

Adding a title that's already in your library from another source asks first, so you don't follow it twice.

Many titles go by several names, like an English and a Japanese title. To catch those too, turn on **Settings → Library → Look Up Alternative Titles**. Titles whose source lists no other names are then looked up on [MangaUpdates](https://www.mangaupdates.com) when you add them or restore a backup. Only the title and author are sent.
