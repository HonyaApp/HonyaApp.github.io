---
title: Migration
description: Move titles to the same manga on another source, keeping progress, categories and notes.
---

Sites come and go, and some have better scans or faster releases than others. Migration moves a title to the same manga on another source, keeping your place.

## Migrate titles

- **One title**: tap **Migrate** on its page.
- **Several at once**: go to **Settings → Migrate**, where your library is listed by source. Choose the source to move away from, then the titles to move.

Choose which sources to search, in order of preference. Honya looks for each title on them and shows the best match. **Also Search Alternative Titles** tries a few of a title's other names when it isn't found by its own; it's slower, but finds more.

For each title, check the match, then:

- **Migrate** to move it,
- **Choose Match** to pick a different result,
- **Search Manually** to look it up yourself, or
- **Skip** to leave it where it is.

## What carries over

Choose what moves with each title:

- **Chapters and History**: read and bookmarked chapters move by chapter number, and every chapter up to the last one read is marked read.
- **Categories**
- **Notes**
- **Custom Cover**
- **Trackers**: the ones kept from a Mihon backup.

**Delete Downloads** removes the old title's downloads once it's moved.
