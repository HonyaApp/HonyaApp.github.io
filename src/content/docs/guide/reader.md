---
title: The reader
description: Reading modes, page layout, cropping, backgrounds, per-title settings and page bookmarks.
---

Tap a chapter to open it. Tap the middle of the screen to show or hide the controls, and the edges to turn pages. The controls have the chapter list, a page slider with thumbnails, the previous and next chapters, and **Reader Settings**.

When you finish a chapter, the next one follows on. Reading progress is saved as you go, page by page.

## Reading modes

- **Paged (right to left)**: for manga, turned like a Japanese book.
- **Paged (left to right)**: for comics and most manhua.
- **Vertical scroll**: one long strip, for webtoons and manhwa.

Set the mode most of your titles use in **Settings → Reader → Default Reading Mode**. Choosing a different mode while reading sets it for that title only.

### Thumbnails in vertical scroll

In vertical scroll, the page thumbnails run in a column down the right side of the screen, and the chapter buttons point up for the previous chapter and down for the next. **Thumbnails**, in **Reader Settings** while reading or in **Settings → Reader → Vertical Scroll**, moves them:

- **Left** or **Right**: a column down that side.
- **Bottom**: a row along the bottom, with the chapter buttons pointing left and right, as in the paged modes.

It applies to every title.

## Page layout

In the paged modes, **Page Layout** chooses how many pages you see:

- **Single**: one page at a time.
- **Double**: two pages side by side. Wide pages, like spreads, still show alone.
- **Auto**: two pages side by side when the screen is wider than it is tall, and one otherwise.

If two-page spreads come out split across the wrong pair, turn on **Shift Pages by One** to move every pair along by a page. If the two halves come out on the wrong sides, turn on **Swap Facing Pages** to swap the pages on each screen.

## Display

- **Crop Borders** trims the white or black margins around scanned pages, so the art fills more of the screen.
- **Background** is black, gray or white, for the space around pages.
- **Keep Screen On** stops the screen dimming while you read. It applies to every title.

## Settings for one title

Page layout, shifting, swapping, background, cropping and the reading mode are defaults: changing one in **Reader Settings** while reading sets it for that title, and other titles keep the defaults from **Settings → Reader**. **Use Defaults for This Title** puts a title back on the defaults.

With iCloud Sync on, both the defaults and each title's own settings are the same on all your devices.

## Bookmarks

- **Page bookmarks**: tap the bookmark button while reading to save the page you're on. They're listed on the title's page and in the reader's chapter list, so you can go straight back.
- **Chapter bookmarks**: bookmark a chapter from the title's chapter list. You can filter the list to bookmarked chapters, and keep them from being deleted with read downloads.

## Share or save a page

Long-press a page to open the share sheet for its image. From there you can send it, save it to Photos, or choose **Set as Cover** to make it the title's cover in Honya.
