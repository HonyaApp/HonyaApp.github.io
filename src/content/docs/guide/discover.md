The **Discover** tab is where to look when you want something new.

## The discovery queue

Each day, Discover picks new titles from your sources that match the genres in your library. Go through them one at a time: add one to your library, or turn it down. Each pick says which of your genres it matches, and **Similar in Your Library** shows the titles you already have that share them.

When you've seen them all, you're caught up. A new queue arrives the next day.

### Choose what it looks for

Open **Settings → Discover** to steer the queue:

- **Interested**: new titles are matched against the ten most common genres in your library. Remove one to stop matching it, or add others.
- **Not Interested**: titles with these genres never come up. Turning a title down in the queue asks which of its genres to add here.

Changes apply from the next day's queue. With iCloud Sync on, the queue, your genres and the titles you turned down are the same on every device.

## Anime seasons

The season banners show the manga behind last season's, this season's and next season's most popular anime. The charts come from [AniList](https://anilist.co), and each title is looked for in your library, then on the sources you use most, so a season opens on titles that are ready to read. Titles none of your sources list are left out.

Use **Options** to filter the banners and change their order. Your choices apply to every season.

## Sources

Below the queue are your installed sources. Choose one to [browse it](/guide/browse-and-search/).
