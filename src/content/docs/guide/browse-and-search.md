## Browse a source

Open **Discover** and choose a source under **Sources** to browse it. You'll see its popular titles first.

**Sort By** lists the orders the site offers, with Honya's **Popular** and **Latest** among them. Where the site's own sort already gives those lists, its options take their place, so the same list isn't offered twice.

**Filters** shows what the source lets you narrow by: genres to include or exclude, status, type, author and so on. Each source has its own, because each site does. Some sources fetch their filters from the site, such as the genres it currently lists; they appear a moment after the screen opens.

If a source can't load, **Retry** tries again and **Open in WebView** shows the site itself, which helps when the site wants you to sign in or pass a check first.

## Search everything

The **Search** tab looks through your library and every installed source at once. Results from your library come first, then each source's as they arrive.

Tap a source's heading to see all of its results, where you can page further and use its filters. Your recent searches are kept below the field until you clear them, except in [incognito mode](/guide/history-and-incognito/).

## Sites that check you're not a bot

Some sites put a Cloudflare check in front of their pages. When a source runs into one, Honya opens the page so you can pass the check, then carries on. The cookies the site gives you are kept, so you won't be asked again for a while.
