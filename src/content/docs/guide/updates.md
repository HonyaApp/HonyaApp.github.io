---
title: Library updates
description: How Honya checks your library for new chapters, automatically or when you ask.
---

Honya checks the titles in your library for new chapters and lists what it finds in **Updates**, which the bell on the Library tab opens. The bell shows how many new chapters there are.

## Check now

Pull down in **Updates**, or tap **Update Library**, to check every title now. **Stop Updating** stops part way; the titles that weren't checked go first next time.

From the list you can open a title, mark chapters read or unread, and download them. Tap **Select** to do that to several at once.

## Automatic updates

Open **Update Settings** in Updates to choose how often Honya checks on its own, from every few hours to weekly, or off.

- **Only on Wi-Fi** and **Only While Charging** limit when automatic updates run.
- iOS decides when apps get to run in the background, so automatic updates can run late. An update that's overdue also runs when you open the app.

## Skip titles

Checking fewer titles makes updates faster and kinder to the sites your sources read from. **Skip Titles** leaves out titles that are:

- **Completed**: the series has ended.
- **With Unread Chapters**: you haven't caught up with what's there already.
- **Not Started**: you haven't read any of it yet.
- **Not Due for a Chapter**: a title is due when its usual release interval, worked out from its recent chapters, has passed.

These apply to every update, including ones you start yourself.

## More with each update

- **Refresh Covers and Details** updates each title's cover, description and status too. It takes an extra request per title, so updates take about twice as long.
- **Download New Chapters** downloads new chapters as soon as they're found, whether by an update or when you open a title.
