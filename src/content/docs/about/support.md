## Send us a note

The best way to reach us is from the app: **Settings → Send Feedback**. Choose whether it's a feature request, a problem, or something else, and tell us what's going on. Screenshots help, especially with a problem, and **Include Device Details** adds your app version, device and iOS version so we can track it down. We read every note.

Sending feedback needs you to be signed in to iCloud on your device.

## Problems with a source

Honya doesn't make, host or maintain sources. If a source stops working or a site's content is wrong, check for an update in **Settings → Sources**, then contact whoever publishes the repository. See [Troubleshooting](/guide/troubleshooting/#a-source-stops-working).

## Common questions

**Where do I get sources?**
From repositories other people publish. Honya doesn't list them. See [How sources work](/sources/how-sources-work/).

**Can I move my library from Mihon, Tachiyomi or Paperback?**
Yes. Make a backup there and restore it in Honya. See [Backup and restore](/guide/backup-and-restore/).

**Is Honya on Android, Mac or the web?**
Honya is for iPhone and iPad. A Mihon backup made in Honya restores in Mihon on Android.

**Does Honya cost anything?**
No. Honya is free to use.
